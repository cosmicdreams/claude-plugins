# T3 conventions

Briefing: pinned review surface. Active means working; unread means a sourced human decision; snoozed means hold; settled means closed; archived means out of view. Beads tracks tasks. A state operation failure stays visible in the report. Notifications remain off until delivery and suppression rules are tested.

Triage title: `[PROJECT] triage · summary · fp <exact-key>`. Keep one thread per project/environment/fingerprint. After review, send count updates to Briefing, preserving the checkpoint. Never infer deployment or resolution from merge auto-settlement.

Status line: evidence timestamp · source coverage/gaps · last success/overdue · decision needed or none established.
